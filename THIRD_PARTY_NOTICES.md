Any license granted for Lidscape's original code does not grant rights to third-party materials.

## Apple model assets

The USDZ files in `Resources/Models/` are Apple assets downloaded from Apple's product pages. They are not covered by Lidscape's code license. Their redistribution rights have not been established. See the source links in the README.

Screenshots in `docs/images/` depict those models; a code license does not independently grant rights to the depicted Apple assets.
